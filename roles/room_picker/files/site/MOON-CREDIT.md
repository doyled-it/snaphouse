# Moon image credit

`moon.jpg` is the full-moon frame from NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio (SVS)
visualization "Moon Phase and Libration, 2015" (SVS 4236), which is rendered from
data of NASA's Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (the LOLA laser altimeter and the LROC
camera). It is a rendered view of the real lunar surface, not a camera photograph of
the sky.

- Source page: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4236/
- Image record: NASA Image and Video Library, id `GSFC_20171208_Archive_e000868`
  ("Full Moon"), https://images.nasa.gov/details/GSFC_20171208_Archive_e000868
- Original file: https://images-assets.nasa.gov/image/GSFC_20171208_Archive_e000868/GSFC_20171208_Archive_e000868~orig.jpg
- Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio. Visualization by Ernie Wright
  (STC). Scientists John Keller and Noah Petro (NASA/GSFC).
- Changes: cropped to the lunar disc with a 1 percent margin and resized to 600 by
  600 pixels (JPEG, about 75 KB). Nothing was retouched.
